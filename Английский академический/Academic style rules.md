#англ_академ 
1) Use formal vocabulary
	Examples:
	- dad -> father
	- like... -> such as...
	- lots -> significant number
	- 3 -> three (all numbers under 10 need to be expressed as words, except with measurement units or mixing words and numbers in one sentence)
2) Use formal words instead of multiword verbs
	Example:
	- go up -> increase
	- talk about -> discuss
	- get better -> improve
	- get worse -> deteriorate
	- bring up -> raised
	- look up to -> admire
	- get in to -> enter
3) Use full form of verbes
	Examples:
	- don't -> do not
	- can't -> cannot
	- won't -> will not
